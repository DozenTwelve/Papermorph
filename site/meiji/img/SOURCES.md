# Picture sources

Every picture on the page, with creator, date, source and licence as stated on the source page. Nothing is re-dated.
Event pictures were converted to WebP at no more than 1200 px on the long side; the page tones them (a light sepia) but
does not alter their content. "Later" marks a picture made after the event it sits beside; the card's caption says so.
Map data: see `../data/LICENSE-map.txt`.
