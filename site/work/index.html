Questions

How long does a chapter take?

It depends on its length and shape. The trial shows how much work your material needs, and the quote comes with dates.

Which languages can the narration be in?

English, Japanese, Chinese and most other major languages. The player itself speaks English, Japanese, Chinese and Russian, and lines in another language are subtitled.

Can it go inside our own site, app or LMS?

Yes. A book is plain static files you can host, link or embed, and it can also be packaged as SCORM or rendered to video. We test on the browsers and devices you name.

How is the content checked?

You approve the script first. Answers are graded by meaning, every step is played through by tests, and each chapter goes through review rounds before you see it.

Is our material kept confidential?

Yes. We can sign an NDA before you send anything, and your material is used only for your project.

Who owns the result?

You do. Public-domain pictures and data keep their own licences, which we list beside them.

常见问题

一章要做多久？

看长度和形态。试做之后就知道你们的素材要多少工作量，报价会附上日期。

配音支持哪些语言？

中文、日语、英语以及大多数主要语言。播放器界面支持中、日、英、俄四种，其他语言的台词配字幕。

能放进我们自己的网站、App 或 LMS 吗？

能。成品是纯静态文件，可以托管、链接或嵌入，也能打包成 SCORM 或导出视频。我们会在你们指定的浏览器和设备上测试。

内容的准确性怎么保证？

脚本先由你们批准。答案按含义判分，每一步都有测试播放，每章交给你们之前都要经过几轮审片。

素材会保密吗？

会。发素材之前可以先签 NDA，素材只用于你们的项目。

成品归谁？

归你们。公版图片和数据保留各自的许可，我们会注明出处。
