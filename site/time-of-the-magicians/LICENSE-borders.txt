  Schvitz, Guy, Luc Girardin, Seraina Rüegger, Nils B. Weidmann, Lars-Erik Cederman and Kristian Skrede Gleditsch.
  2022. "Mapping the International System, 1886-2019: The CShapes 2.0 Dataset." Journal of Conflict Resolution 66(1).
  ETH Zürich, International Conflict Research. https://icr.ethz.ch/data/cshapes/
