    Sources and credits

    The story follows Wolfram Eilenberger, Time of the Magicians: The Great Decade of Philosophy, 1919–1929 (2018; English translation by Shaun Whiteside, Penguin 2020), told here in our own words. Dates and places not in the book, and every “Europe in” event, were checked against Wikipedia (English and German) and, for some dates, the Deutsche Biographie, the Metzler philosophers' lexicon (spektrum.de), Russell: the Journal of Bertrand Russell Studies and the Goethe-Institut. Facts we could not confirm in a second source were left out.

    Borders: CShapes 2.0, ETH Zürich (Schvitz, Girardin, Rüegger, Weidmann, Cederman and Gleditsch, 2022), one snapshot for 1 July of each year, under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (notice). The Saar, the Memel territory and the 1922 split of Upper Silesia are not in the data.
