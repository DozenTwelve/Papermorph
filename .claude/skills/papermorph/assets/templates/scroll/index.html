Nineteen Cities

Papermorph: a scroll story

Where the world's biggest cities are, on a free map of the world.

Scroll down to start; scroll back to rewind.

↓

Natural Earth, a public-domain map of the world, draws it as 177 countries.

Nineteen of its cities have ten million people or more, counting the whole urban area around each.

Each circle's area is the city's people.

Ten of the nineteen are in Asia: Tokyo and Osaka, Shanghai and Beijing, Manila, Dhaka, Kolkata, Delhi, Mumbai and Karachi.

And one is far ahead. Tokyo: about 36 million people, nearly twice New York, the next.

Sources and credits
